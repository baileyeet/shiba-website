An independent research organization based in Tokyo, focused on AI safety.

We are an independent research organization based in Tokyo and focused on AI safety. Our group researches multi-agent systems safety, AI governance, and white box interpretability. In addition to these topics, we are interested in projects related to metacognition, persona-based alignment and AI security.
